## Overview
Your final project will be to produce something interactive (a vibe-coded website, a board game or
physical puzzle, etc.) that can help a user understand an aspect of this course that you found
difficult. You will be evaluated on how your final product guides a user to some insight that you
think is important in becoming unblocked in understanding a concept. (Ideally because that was
the insight that helped *you* understand the concept yourself!)

Identify the topic of your project (a definition, a fact, a method, an algorithm, ...) and explain,
as best as you can, why that topic was or is difficult for you to understand. "Because it's confusing"
won't work. Here are some examples:
* you might say that your topic is whatever-first-search, and you think it's difficult
because it's hard to infer the big picture of where the algorithm will explore given the local specification
of what gets put in the bag
* you might say your topic is base cases in recursive backtracking, and you think it's difficult
to understand because the base cases don't always correspond exactly with something given in the
problem statement (for example, the change-making problem has base cases beyond just the denominations of coins).

## Project Timeline

### Draft + Feedback
Monday, 11/23: share a project draft in class. Bring a laptop or something else that allows you to share a prototype
of your planned . 1 point for having any prototype ready (could even ), 1 point for giving feedback to a few other people (collected in class).
### Final sharing of project
Friday, 12/4: final project sharing day. Bring a laptop or your final physical item. 2 point for feedback,
6 points for project. You will be evaluated on your ability to explain to Lucy or a TA:
* What topic did you choose and what about that topic did you initially find challenging?
* How did you arrive at your design? Did you try anything that didn't work out?
* What insight are you trying to help a user arrive at?
* What aspect of your design is most successful in helping the user arrive at that insight?
* Are there any aspects of your design that you would change to help guide your user to your insight?
* What did you learn in the process?
